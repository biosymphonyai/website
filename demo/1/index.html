Orchestrating the signals of life

A virtual tissue for target discovery.

Biosymphony builds multimodal, multi-scale models of living tissue — causal and mechanistic, not correlational. We simulate how a perturbation travels from molecule to cell to tissue, so the target you carry into the clinic is the one that holds.

The thesis

The cell was never the unit of disease. The tissue is.

A hepatocyte in a dish and a hepatocyte in a fibrotic liver are the same cell running a different program. Virtual-cell models learn the cell and lose the context. We model the context: the neighbours, the gradients, the matrix, and the feedback that makes a tissue behave like an organ instead of a population.

Four commitments
01

Multimodal

Transcriptomic, proteomic, epigenomic, spatial and imaging channels enter one latent space instead of four disconnected pipelines.

02

Multi-scale

Molecule, cell, neighbourhood, tissue. One model carries state across all four, so a pathway edit shows up as a tissue phenotype.

03

Causal

Trained on perturbation, not observation. The model answers what happens if you intervene — not what tends to co-occur.

04

Mechanistic

Every prediction resolves to a pathway a biologist can read, argue with, and design an experiment against.

What we are building
Targets

Target discovery in context

We rank targets by their simulated effect on tissue state, not by differential expression in a bulk sample. Context is the filter most target lists never pass through.

Data

Multi-omics, one substrate

Public atlases, licensed cohorts, and purpose-built perturbation screens are fused into a single representation — the value is in the joins, not the volume.

Translation

Built against failure modes

Phase II attrition is a modelling problem wearing a clinical costume. We validate against the mechanisms that actually break translation.
