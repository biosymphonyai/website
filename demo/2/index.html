Computable biology

Computable models of disease to cut drug trial failures.

Nine in ten drugs that enter the clinic never leave it. The models that picked those targets were built on correlation in the wrong tissue at the wrong scale. We are building the ones that aren't.

The thesis

Attrition is not bad luck. It is a modelling failure you can measure.

Every failed Phase II was preceded by a prediction someone believed. The interesting question is not which trials failed but which class of model produced the belief — and whether a multi-scale, causal, mechanistic model would have produced a different one.

Four commitments
01

Multimodal

One representation across transcriptomics, proteomics, epigenomics and spatial imaging.

02

Multi-scale

Molecular events propagate to cellular states and on to tissue-level phenotype.

03

Causal

Perturbation-trained, so interventions are predicted rather than inferred.

04

Mechanistic

Predictions come with the pathway that produced them, not just a score.

What we are building
Platform

A computational tech-bio stack

Data fusion, representation learning, perturbation simulation and target ranking as one system rather than four vendors.

Discovery

Targets that survive context

Candidate targets are scored by simulated tissue response, then triaged for tractability and translational risk before a single wet experiment runs.

Programmes

Indication-led

We start from an indication where tissue context is decisive and where existing target lists have visibly failed.
