Computable biology

One model. Every scale.

Two marks, two palettes and two stories were competing. So is the science: molecular models over here, cell atlases over there, tissue simulation somewhere else entirely. Our bet is that the resolution is the same in both cases — put it in one thing.

The thesis

The parts were never the problem. The seams were.

Multi-omics fusion, perturbation modelling and tissue simulation are usually three teams with three data formats and three definitions of success. Every handoff between them loses mechanism. We built one model across the whole span for the same reason you would rather have one instrument than three that nearly agree.

Four commitments
01

Multimodal

Four measurement types, one latent space. The fusion is the product.

02

Multi-scale

Molecular to tissue without a handoff, so a pathway edit reads out as a phenotype.

03

Causal

Perturbation-trained. Ask it what an intervention does and it answers that question.

04

Mechanistic

Attribution to pathway and interaction, by default, on every prediction.

What we are building
Platform

One system, not a pipeline

Data fusion, representation, simulation and ranking share weights and a single evaluation. Improvements compound instead of colliding.

Discovery

Target discovery in tissue context

Candidates ranked by simulated tissue response. Context-dependent targets are found; context-fragile ones are eliminated.

Focus

Computational first

We are a computational tech-bio platform. Wet-lab work exists to falsify the model, on a schedule the model sets.

Resolved, not redecorated.
