Disease, rewritten

Write it down, and you can rewrite it.

Notation was the technology that let music become cumulative — a thing you could hand to a stranger and have them reproduce it exactly. Biology still mostly lacks that. We are building the notation for tissue behaviour, and then the means to edit it.

The thesis

A cell is a note. A tissue is the passage. Disease is the passage played wrong.

This is a metaphor doing real work. Notes only mean something in sequence and in company: the same pitch is consonant or dissonant depending on what surrounds it. Cells are identical in this respect — which is why models that score cells in isolation keep producing targets that evaporate in vivo.

Four commitments
01

Multimodal

Transcript, protein, chromatin and spatial context are four staves of the same score. Read together or not at all.

02

Multi-scale

Phrase, passage, movement. Molecular change has to be legible as tissue behaviour or it has not been modelled.

03

Causal

We learn from what happens when you change a note — perturbation data, not observation alone.

04

Mechanistic

Every prediction can be traced back through the parts that produced it. A result you cannot follow is a result you cannot use.

What we are building
Discovery

Target discovery with the context intact

Targets are ranked on simulated tissue response. The candidate that only works in a dissociated cell is caught before it costs a programme.

Data

Multi-omics, joined properly

Fusing modalities is where most of the signal is created and most of it is lost. We treat the join as the hard part, because it is.

People

A small group, a long horizon

Computational tech-bio in Vancouver. Modelling people who read biology, biologists who read models.

Come help write it down.

We are early, deliberately small, and looking for people who find the tissue scale more interesting than the cell scale.
