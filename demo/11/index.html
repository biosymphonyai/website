The programme for a living tissue.

Programme notes exist because the audience deserves to know what is about to happen and why it is arranged that way. This page is ours: what we are building, at what scale, and what would count as it working.

The thesis

A tissue is an arrangement, and arrangement is the thing we model.

Cells in a tissue are positioned, coupled, and constrained. Those three facts account for most of the behaviour that matters therapeutically and almost none of what a per-cell model retains. Biosymphony models the arrangement — multimodal, multi-scale, causal, mechanistic — and reads therapeutic hypotheses out of it.

Four commitments
I

Multimodal

Transcriptomic, proteomic, epigenomic and spatial measurement, read as one score rather than four.

II

Multi-scale

Molecular through tissue in one continuous model, so an edit at one scale is legible at the next.

III

Causal

Learned from perturbation, so the model answers interventional questions rather than associative ones.

IV

Mechanistic

Every prediction arrives with the pathway that produced it, in a form a biologist can dispute.

What we are building
Programme

The virtual tissue

The substrate: a model of cells in context that can be perturbed cheaply and repeatedly before anything touches a bench.

Movement

Target discovery

Multi-omics target discovery scored on simulated tissue response, tractability and translational risk.

Coda

Translation

The measure of the work is whether it changes which molecules reach patients — and whether they work when they do.
