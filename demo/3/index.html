Computable biology

The tissue is the unit of disease.

A single-cell atlas tells you who is in the room. It does not tell you what the room is doing.

The thesis

We are writing the score, not cataloguing the players.

Cell atlases were a necessary decade. They gave the field an inventory. But an inventory is not a mechanism, and a cell type is not a phenotype — the disease lives in the coordination between cells, in gradients and contacts and feedback that no per-cell embedding retains. Biosymphony models that coordination directly.

Four commitments
i

Multimodal

Expression, protein, chromatin state and spatial position are different notations for one underlying system. We read them as one.

ii

Multi-scale

From molecular interaction to cellular state to tissue architecture — a continuous model rather than three that hand off.

iii

Causal

Built from perturbation data so that the model can be asked a counterfactual and give a defensible answer.

iv

Mechanistic

Interpretability is not a feature we added. A prediction without a mechanism is not evidence.

What we are building
Method

Perturbation as the primary datum

Observational omics describe a steady state. Perturbation data describes the system's response function — the only thing that generalises to a therapeutic intervention.

Discovery

Target discovery, in tissue

We simulate the intervention in a modelled tissue and read out the phenotype. Targets that only work in dissociated cells are visible as such, early.

Rigour

Falsifiable by construction

Every model release ships with the held-out perturbations it failed on. Progress is measured against that list.
