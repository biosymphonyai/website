cells → tissues → organs

Biology, resolved as a signal.

A living tissue is a system under continuous perturbation, emitting measurements at four scales at once. Treat it as a signal-processing problem and most of the modern AI stack becomes applicable — including the parts that require you to be honest about resolution.

The thesis

One model, carrying state from molecule to organ.

The scale boundary is where the field keeps losing information. Molecular models stop at the membrane; cell models stop at the cell; tissue models start from summary statistics that already threw the mechanism away. We are building the model that does not hand off at the boundary.

Four commitments
01

multimodal

Six measurement channels, one latent state. Missing modalities are inferred rather than dropped.

02

multi-scale

State propagates upward and constraints propagate downward. Both directions, one set of weights.

03

causal

Perturbation-conditioned training. The forward pass is an intervention, not a lookup.

04

mechanistic

Attribution back to pathway and cell-cell interaction — required output, not an interpretability afterthought.

What we are building
stack

Infrastructure first

Fusion, tokenisation, training and evaluation for perturbation biology, built as a platform because the bottleneck was never the architecture.

targets

Target discovery as inference

Ranking targets is a query against the model, not a separate pipeline. New data improves every prior answer.

eval

Held-out perturbations

We evaluate on interventions the model has never seen, in tissues it has never seen. Everything else is memorisation with extra steps.

Building the world model for living tissue.
